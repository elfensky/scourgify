<objective>
Prove the whole Phase-2 architecture end-to-end on the thinnest real write verb: **Re-derive status**
(staleness) from the toolbar menu — menu click → PLAN job → picker → EXECUTE job →
`common.write_ops` → diff-after result dialog → library-view refresh — with the write-run lock
mirrored into the menu's greying.

Purpose: retire the plumbing risk (the injected write transport, the Qt-free job layer, the
picker/result contract, the `GuardrailError`-to-result conversion, the refresh call) on a verb whose
whole compute is two column reads and one `set_field` op, so classify's high-stakes engine picker
lands on proven rails instead of inventing them.

Output: `plugin/jobs.py`, `plugin/picker.py`, `plugin/result_dialog.py`, a live `Re-derive status`
menu slot, the `write=` seam on `staleness.write`, and CI coverage of the job bodies with no Calibre
and no GUI.
</objective>

  slice is `compute()` → one `set_field` op, exercisable end-to-end against
  `tests/fixture_db.py` for free.
- "Build classify first" is an instruction about which verb's **UI design risk** to retire first
  among the verbs. It is honoured: classify is the first verb built on the tracer's rails, before
  the deterministic wrangle verb and before synopsis/promote.
- RESEARCH's "seams first" is honoured *inside* the tracer: task 1 lands the `write=` seam on the
